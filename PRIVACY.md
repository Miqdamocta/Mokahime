# 🐾 Mokahime Bot - Privacy Policy 🐾

Your privacy is very important to Mokahime! We believe that your data should be treated with the same care as a favorite treat. Please read how we handle your information, nyaaa~!

## 1. 🌸 Information We Collect
To provide the best experience, Mokahime collects the following limited information:
- **User & Server Identifiers:** We store your Discord User ID and Server ID so Mokahime knows where she is and who she is talking to.
- **Message Content:** Mokahime processes the messages sent as commands to her so she can respond correctly.
- **Public Profile Data:** We may access your public Discord username and avatar to make interactions more personal and cute!

## 2. ✨ How We Use Your Information
Your data is used exclusively for the following:
- **Functionality:** To execute commands and provide the bot's features.
- **Improvement:** To analyze how the bot is used and make Mokahime even smarter and more helpful.
- **Safety:** To prevent abuse and ensure the bot is used according to the Terms of Service.

## 3. 🍪 Data Storage & Security
Mokahime takes security very seriously!
- **No Selling:** We will NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties. That would be very mean! 😿
- **Secure Storage:** Data is stored using secure methods to prevent unauthorized access.
- **Retention:** We only keep data as long as it is necessary for the bot to function.

## 4. 🎀 Third-Party Sharing
Mokahime does not share your data with outside companies. The only exception is the necessary communication with Discord's own API to make the bot work.

## 5. 🐾 Your Rights & Control
You are in control of your data!
- **Removal:** You can stop the collection of your data by removing Mokahime from your server.
- **Contact:** If you have questions about your data, you can contact the bot owner (Moka).
